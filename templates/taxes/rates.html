Important Notes

  • · All taxes are in Nigerian Naira (₦) and inclusive of applicable rates.
  • · Taxes are applied per departing passenger unless otherwise stated.
  • · Infants (under 2) may be exempt from certain charges — see airline policy.
  • · Transit passengers staying less than 24 hours may attract reduced charges.
  • · Rates are subject to change by government order. Check before travel.

Where Do the Taxes Go?

  • · VAT (7.5%): Remitted to the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS).
  • · ADL: Funds infrastructure development at Nigerian airports (FAAN).
  • · PSC: Retained by NAIA to fund terminal operations and passenger amenities.
  • · Security Fee: Funds screening equipment and security personnel.
  • · Departure Tax: Federal government consolidated revenue fund.

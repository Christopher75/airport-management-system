What Are Airport Taxes?

Government Levies

Mandated by the Federal Government of Nigeria. Includes VAT (7.5%), Departure Tax, and Customs Stamp Duty — remitted to the FIRS.

Airport Charges

Retained by Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport for infrastructure maintenance. Includes Passenger Service Charge (PSC) and Airport Development Levy (ADL).

Security Fees

Covers airport security operations, screening equipment, and safety personnel. Applied per departing passenger as mandated by NCAA.

Important Notes

  • All fees are payable in Nigerian Naira (₦) before aircraft departure.
  • Parking fees are calculated on actual departure time, not scheduled time.
  • Outstanding balances may result in aircraft hold until full settlement.
  • GPU and PCA services are billed per actual hours of use.
  • Weekly rates apply for stays exceeding 7 consecutive days.
  • Cargo and maintenance stands are subject to separate agreements.
